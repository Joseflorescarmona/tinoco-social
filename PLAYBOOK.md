## Rules from now on

1. **Every Reel and Story ends with the tester call** to tinocokids.com/testers. Keep it gentle: one line, adults with Android.
2. **USA first.** English captions are written for US parents, teachers, SLPs and OTs (speech and occupational therapists). Follow and comment on US accounts in those groups and in autism or special-education spaces.
3. **Keep Spain warm:** at least one Spanish Story or post a week. The Spanish autism groups get Spanish DMs and comments.
4. **Lead with the games that hold players:** draw, city, shop, detective. Wild, science and letters go to the back of the queue.
5. **Timing:** post at about 21:00 Spain time (afternoon in the US). Try US evening, 01:00 to 03:00 Spain time, when it can be scheduled.
6. **One Reel every 1 to 2 days**, plus a tester-progress Story whenever the count changes.

## Data review, 28 Nov (Meta Business Suite + site stats)

What the numbers say:
1. **Reels reach new people; photo posts don't.** Most Reels reach 100–130 accounts (mostly non-followers). Photo posts reach 12–24, which is only some of our own followers.
2. **Two Reels flopped:** Draw (23 Nov) reached 9 and My Day (20 Nov) reached 15. Cause unknown; watch whether it repeats.
3. **People leave Reels fast:** average watch is 4–9 s on 12–21 s Reels. The first 2 seconds decide everything.
4. **Posts bring no followers directly:** "Follows" from posts is 0. New followers come from what we do: following, commenting, replying.
5. **Stories with a tester call bring visitors to the site; Reels mostly don't.** Site visits jump on Story days: 11 Nov (95 opens), 16 Nov (ES Story, 45), 20 Nov (bio Story, 40 US), 21 Nov (7/12 Story), 27 Nov (11/12 Story, 61 US). The Dino Reel with no tester call brought 1. My Day reached only 15 on Reels, but its Story brought 40 US visits.
6. **The US comes when we post Stories with a link and talk to US accounts:** 27–28 Nov, 61 + 39 US opens, after the Story, the comments and the follows.
7. **Tester count moves after progress Stories + reminders:** 7 (21 Nov) → 10 (25 Nov) → 11 (27 Nov).
8. **Follows:** 207 in 28 days, but 11–22 Nov were mostly follow-for-follow accounts that left again. Real, targeted growth is +2 to +6 a day.

Decisions (small steps every day):
- **Every day:** 1 Reel or carousel scheduled in Business Suite; 15–20 targeted follows; 5–8 genuine comments; reply to everyone.
- **Every Reel:** text hook on the first frame (a problem plus a promise), at most about 12 s, best games first (Draw, City, Shop).
- **Stories are for testers and visits:** a progress Story whenever the count changes, always pointing to the bio link.
- **Test this week:** Sunday's carousel (saves and shares) against Reels (reach).
- **Change course when the data says so:** if something doesn't move a number in a week, drop it.

## Round routine (each round)
1. **Data first.** Business Suite → Content: reach, views, follows and shares of the latest posts. Insights: daily follows and profile visits. Site `/api/stats`: opens by country since the last post. Play Console: testers. Write one line in the scorecard below.
2. Inboxes (all 3) + comments → reply.
3. Follow-backs from the last batch → follow 15–20 new (Paige-type lists).
4. 5–8 comments on fresh posts in our community.
5. Schedule the next post or Story in Business Suite.
6. Decide: what worked since the last round? Keep it. What didn't? Change one thing.
