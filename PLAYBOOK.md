# Instagram playbook

What the numbers say and what we do about it. Update after each data check.

## What the data showed (23 Nov 2026, last 30 days)

Source: `tinocokids.com/api/stats` (anonymous counts; a session is one game opened, not one person).

- 403 sessions: Spain 324, USA 76. No other country reached 10.
- USA showed up on only two days, both with a tester call:
  - 11 Nov (36 sessions): first Reels plus a tester caption.
  - 20 Nov (40 sessions): My Day Reel plus the Story pointing to the bio link.
- Spain spiked on post days too: 11 Nov (59), the Spanish tester Story on 16 Nov (45) and 21 Nov (24).
- The plain Dino Reel with no tester call (18 Nov) brought almost nobody.
- Visits only come on post days: 1 to 3 sessions on the days after.
- 56% of sessions are quick exits (the player leaves within seconds).
- Most played (minutes): draw 252, city 91, shop 70, detective 62, band 39. Barely played: wild, science, letters.

## Rules from now on

1. **Every Reel and Story ends with the tester call** to tinocokids.com/testers. Keep it gentle: one line, adults with Android.
2. **USA first.** English captions are written for US parents, teachers, SLPs and OTs (speech and occupational therapists). Follow and comment on US accounts in those groups and in autism or special-education spaces.
3. **Keep Spain warm:** at least one Spanish Story or post a week. The Spanish autism groups get Spanish DMs and comments.
4. **Lead with the games that hold players:** draw, city, shop, detective. Wild, science and letters go to the back of the queue.
5. **Timing:** post at about 21:00 Spain time (afternoon in the US). Try US evening, 01:00 to 03:00 Spain time, when it can be scheduled.
6. **One Reel every 1 to 2 days**, plus a tester-progress Story whenever the count changes.
