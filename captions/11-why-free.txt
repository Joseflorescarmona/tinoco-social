Why Tinoco is free 💛

I'm a dad of autistic kids, and I work with technology every day. I know how most kids' games are built: to keep children hooked, with flashing lights, loud sounds, ads, timers and "buy more" buttons.

My kids needed the opposite. Calm screens, a clear friendly voice, no rush, no pressure. Games that give them what they need, and never too much.

So I built Tinoco for them. It's made to be put down, not to hook: a small adventure, then it's done. Your child learns, enjoys and relaxes, and you can say yes without worrying.

It started for my kids. Now it's for every child who needs a calm moment. And it's free, because calm, safe games shouldn't be a luxury.
