Android families wanted! 📱

Tinoco is coming to Google Play, and before it can go live Google asks for a 2-week test with real families.

Who: parents and other adults (18+) with an Android phone.

What you'd do:
1️⃣ Go to tinocokids.com/testers (link in our bio) and tap "Join the testers". One tap with your Google account, nothing to type
2️⃣ When the test opens, tap the Install button on that same page
3️⃣ Open it now and then, and keep it installed for 14 days

It's free, there are no ads, and your feedback helps us make it better.

Thank you! 💛
