Why Tinoco exists 💛

I'm a dad of autistic kids. Every game they liked was full of ads, pop-ups and "buy more" buttons. I got tired of it.

So I started making my own games for them at home: calm, clear, no ads, no tricks. That became Tinoco.

Now it's free for every family. No ads, no purchases, no data sold.
