Six text slides: Why Tinoco is free. A dad of autistic kids who works in tech explains that most kids' games are built to hook children with flashing lights, ads and timers. His kids needed calm screens and no pressure, so he built Tinoco. No ads, no purchases, no timers, no accounts. Reading, counting, drawing and music, a voice that reads aloud, a daily adventure with a real end. Free, because calm, safe games shouldn't be a luxury.
